# 0004 — Artifact lifecycle

**Status:** open.

## Question

When should a brief, proposal, research report, architecture document, decision,
plan or handoff be created, promoted, archived or superseded?

## Current hypothesis

Keep the Issue as the source of task intent and acceptance criteria. Use a
proposal for alternatives before implementation, research for evidence, a
decision record after an owner-approved choice, a plan for long-running work,
and a handoff for resumable execution state. Retire or mark superseded material
when it no longer guides current work.

The promotion rules, archival policy and required cross-links still need a
separate design and pilot.
